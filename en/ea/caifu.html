Overview

Trend Star is a trend-following scalper built around the M5 timeframe. It uses four EMAs (13/21/34/55) as the base trend read, with a seven-dimension confluence filter as the entry gate; it scales up by multiplier in trend direction to amplify gains, and grids in against the trend, layer by layer, to average down cost. Average-price take-profit, a trailing stop, and a global-dollar close-all round out a complete entry → scaling → exit → defense loop.

Data on this page comes from live demo-account trade records, covering 2026-02-03 to 2026-06-02 (119 days) on XAUUSD, 7,626 trades total, with max drawdown around 8.1%. Past performance does not indicate future results and is for reference only.

Key features

Seven-dimension confluence filter

CCI/DeMarker, Bulls/Bears, Gator, MACD, ADX, KDJ, fractals — seven filters, each independently toggleable, to tune the entry rhythm to your preference.

Trend + counter-trend dual module

Trend direction scales up with momentum, counter-trend direction grids in to average down — built to handle both trending and ranging markets.

Average-price take-profit + trailing stop

Average-price take-profit locks in overall profit; the trailing stop follows new highs, giving dual protection.

Global dollar close-all

Once the set global-profit dollar amount is hit, the whole batch exits and locks in the gain; an optional global max-loss cap (off by default) can also be enabled.

Batch cooldown

After a full close, the EA enters a cooldown period to avoid chasing straight back in and stacking up consecutive losses.

Max spread and lot limits

A max-spread parameter filters extreme quotes; a max-lot parameter caps how large any single scaled-in trade can get.

Entry logic

The system reads trend direction from the alignment of EMA13/21/34/55, then the enabled filters vote together on momentum, strength, and oscillation conditions; only when multiple filters confirm together is a signal treated as valid.

CheckDescription
Spread filterChecks live spread against the max-spread limit before entry; pauses new orders if exceeded.
Cooldown protectionNo new orders open within the cooldown window after a full close, avoiding an immediate re-entry.

Position management

The trend direction scales up layer by layer at the set spacing and trend-scaling multiplier to amplify gains; the counter-trend direction grids in layer by layer at the grid spacing and counter-trend multiplier to average down cost. Layer count and per-trade lot size on both sides are bounded by the max-lot parameter; the system has no default cap on scaling layers, so actual risk grows the longer a move continues — assess your own capital tolerance accordingly.

Exit and risk-control priority

The system runs multiple stop-loss and profit-protection layers, checked top-down every cycle.
