Others give you a screenshot. We give you data.

Most EA sellers hand you an equity-curve screenshot. We give you: how this exact strategy performed in 2024, 2025 and 2026 separately, what the max drawdown was, what capital and lot size produced it, and a demo account you can log into and check yourself.

We check what others don't

Our in-house validation pipeline: a tick-level real-quote database, an automated performance-verification pipeline, and a full scan of 177 EA source codes. We also do something most competitors skip — we read every EA's risk-control code line by line to confirm it actually fires. In EAs circulating on the market we've found stop-loss parameters listed in the settings panel but never referenced in code, and account-level stop conditions that are always false. More than once.

The library keeps getting smaller

Because of that process, our library isn't growing — it's shrinking, from over a hundred candidates down to the 7 we sell today. If it doesn't pass our own bar, it doesn't go on the shelf.

Most recent delisting: August 2026 — a laddered-entry strategy failed a continuous-validation review (most starting points couldn't survive the full run) and was pulled from the library.
